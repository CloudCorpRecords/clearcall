    The 3 rules that stop almost every scam

    1. Slow down. Urgency is the scammer's main tool. Real problems can wait an hour.
    2. Never pay with gift cards, Bitcoin, wire, or cash apps — and never share a 6-digit code. No real organisation asks for these. Ever.
    3. Use a number you already trust. Call the number on your bank card, your bill, or your family's contact card — never a number from the suspicious message.

    🔒 Your privacy: ClearCall runs entirely on this page, on your device. The message you paste is never sent anywhere, saved, or shared. Close the tab and it is gone.

    ClearCall is a helper, not a guarantee. If you already sent money or shared a code, call your bank immediately using the number on your card, and tell a family member or your local police non-emergency line. In the US you can also report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
